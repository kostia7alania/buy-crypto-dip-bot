## Research method

Three read-only subagents each owned 41 IDs:

1. R001–R041 — database, tenant isolation, auth, audit, and Gate governance.
2. R042–R082 — runtime, market data, risk evidence, Bybit Demo, and reconciliation.
3. R083–R123 — product truth, UX, SEO, analytics, market, support, privacy, and legal readiness.

Each agent ran twice. Loop 1 built the evidence map and backlog. Loop 2 checked current references, removed cross-lane duplicates, made dependencies acyclic, and rewrote acceptance criteria as observable outcomes.
