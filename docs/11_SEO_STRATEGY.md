# SEO and public content

Public acquisition routes are defined by `packages/seo-keywords/src/index.ts`
and the Nuxt pages. Keep canonical URLs, page intent, titles, structured data
and internal links aligned. Do not turn a configured pair, a future provider
integration or a source checkbox into a published support claim.

Remote main prerenders public routes while the private dashboard is CSR and
noindex; the recovery checkout still has its older rendering setup. Preserve
the static-public/dynamic-private boundary when integrating.

The [claims registry](21_CLAIMS_REGISTRY.md) governs source-specific copy.
Canonical keyword mapping, topic hierarchy, sourced comparisons and deployed
structured-data validation remain open or partial (R102-R108/R117). Analytics
consent is implemented in the local line; measured acquisition/conversion
outcomes are not established. See [the backlog](../tasks/00_MASTER_PLAN.md).
